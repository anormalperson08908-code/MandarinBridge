FAQs

Frequently asked questions help students understand how to use MandarinBridge.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Creating an account saves your level, lesson progress, and quiz history across visits.

How do quizzes work?

Quizzes reinforce recent lessons. Take them anytime from the dashboard to see what to review next.

Can I track progress?

Open Progress to view completion bars and reports for vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation.
