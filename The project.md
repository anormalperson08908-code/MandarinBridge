# Topic Selection: MandarinBridge

## Selected Topic
**Experiment Topic 4:** International Student Chinese Immersion Learning Platform "MandarinBridge"

## Project Description
MandarinBridge is designed to solve the "language bubble" challenge faced by international students in China. While traditional apps focus on rote memorization, this platform emphasizes **immersion**. 

### Why This Topic?
1. **Practical Utility:** Addresses the real-world need for students to navigate daily life and social interactions in a Chinese-speaking environment.
2. **Contextual Learning:** Focuses on situational dialogue and cultural nuances rather than isolated vocabulary.
3. **Bridge to Integration:** Acts as a digital mentor to help students transition from classroom learning to real-world application, fostering deeper cultural exchange.
